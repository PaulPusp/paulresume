Method
V(r)=Vmax·√[(R/r)^1.5 · exp(1-(R/r)^1.5)]
Right-of-track wind receives a 15% Northern Hemisphere asymmetry boost.
surge=[0.0105(1010-Pc)+2.5(V/150)^2·side·speed·mangrove]·shelf
Shelf factor = clamp(√(30/avgSeaDepth),0.7,1.8); surge capped at 10 m. Coastal water level = surge hydrograph + tide + sea-level rise. The generic embankment proxy uses crest = local elevation + slider height; it does not represent real polders.
rain water=(rain/1000)·(1-0.7·drainage)·rainFactor
OSM river-corridor cells receive the existing discharge term; water relaxes locally downslope for 3 iterations.
finalDepth=max(C,P)+0.3·min(C,P)
The 0.3 interaction term is an illustrative compound-flood proxy, not a calibrated hydraulic law.
Df: 0→0, .5→.2, 1→.4, 2→.7, 3+→.9
Dw=clamp(((V-90)/130)^2,0,1); D=1-(1-Dw)(1-Df)
Settlements use OSM population where available; otherwise city=200,000 and town=20,000 are presentation proxies. Cropland is a proxy: land <10 m outside 5 km of a loaded settlement.
DEM: AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM-class), known to be biased high over vegetated/built deltas, so flood depth there is likely underestimated. Roads/settlements/hospitals/rivers/borders: OpenStreetMap contributors. Imagery: Esri. Shelf factor, embankment, mangrove are generic tunable proxies. Not calibrated or predictive.
