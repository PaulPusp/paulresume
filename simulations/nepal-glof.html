Method

DEM is sink-filled, then D8 flow direction and accumulation are derived. The Event-A start follows the DEM route from the source toward Rasuwagadhi. The hydrograph is triangular with T=2V/Qp. Routing uses Manning uniform-flow stage and kinematic celerity c=(5/3)V. HAND is used only as a lateral inundation visualisation. Bank candidates use τ=ρghS and stream power from discharge, slope and width. The landslide screen uses DEM slope plus an optional S/SE/SW aspect filter; red cells additionally require the Event-A hydraulic thresholds. This is a screening display, not a landslide susceptibility probability or predictive hydraulic model. The blue river ribbon is a display aid following the computed D8 thalweg, not a measured river width. The source lake is shown as an area-equivalent circle derived from the reported 0.75 km² lake area, not as the observed lake outline. The optional MODIS snow layer is visual context and is not used in routing.

Presentation caveats

  • 03:15 NPT is the reported Rasuwagadhi impact time, not the observed lake-release time.
  • Flood depth is sensitive to the coarse DEM and simplified cross-sections.
  • Orange markers are threshold exceedances, not observed landslide locations.
  • No factor of safety, pore pressure or susceptibility probability is computed.
  • Landslide screening parameters are literature-guided conditioning variables; they are not calibrated to the 2025 inventory.
